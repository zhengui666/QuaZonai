## Required business evidence

1. Registered original data and matured training labels produce native calibrated factor coefficients; changes retain their true ensemble version and lineage.
2. Shared-account candidate evaluation, cash and incumbent comparisons use matched windows, starting capital, costs and accounting. A high scalar metric, COMPUTED result or empirical frontier does not by itself qualify a candidate.
3. All scientific candidates and capital tests are recorded within the owner's budget. Deterministic recovery of original evidence is identified separately and actual compute is accounted for. Exhaustion closes new experiments while retaining the conclusion opportunity.
4. Capital changes which cross trading or cost boundaries trigger configuration evaluation. A frozen-target capital replay is useful conditional evidence but does not claim joint capital/configuration optimization or real existing-account HOLD performance.
5. New admitted data/labels/account/cost facts create new cycles. Stale basis, expired evidence and unknown receipts cannot publish duplicate or outdated targets.
6. Authoritative outcomes distinguish reception, fills, cash flows and returns, and enter future learning only at their actual availability. Retirement retains responsibility for outstanding commands and positions.
7. Unsupported data, market or account capabilities have explicit evidence gaps. Such a refusal must not substitute for completing a genuine supported positive path.

Detailed counterexamples and coverage are tracked in [the reviewed acceptance set](../quant-pipeline-integrity/design/acceptance.md) and the implementation's `acceptance-coverage.md`. Record actual passed, failed, skipped and unavailable work. Synthetic catalogs and Faux establish controlled software/science behavior; they do not establish profitability or paid-model quality.
